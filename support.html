Support the project

Both tools are free, and they stay free. If one saved you money or time, you can chip in.

I make Steam Value Checker and FanaticalGrabber on my own and pay for the hosting out of pocket. Tips go toward the server bill and the time I spend fixing things. Nothing is locked behind them, and nobody gets treated differently for giving.
