From checkout to a priced library in three steps.

FanaticalGrabber exports what you bought. Steam Value Checker reads that file.

  1. Export your keys

    Open your Fanatical orders, press Reveal All, then export to CSV, JSON, TXT or Markdown.

  2. Send the file to the bot

    Run /value with the file attached, or /library import to keep your games on record. Only the game titles are read.

  3. See the value, then set alerts

    Get MSRP, cheapest price, all-time low and what you saved. Add the games you still want to your wishlist and the bot tells you when they drop.

Questions people ask

Are the tools free?

Yes. Steam Value Checker and FanaticalGrabber are free to use today. If that ever changes for a feature, it will say so on the tool's own page before you use it.

Do I have to give you my Steam or Fanatical password?

No. FanaticalGrabber works on the Fanatical pages you already have open and signed in. Steam Value Checker reads public data, such as a public Steam wishlist, and the web dashboard signs you in through Discord.

Where does my data go?

FanaticalGrabber keeps everything in your browser. Steam Value Checker stores only what its features need, such as your wishlist and alert settings, and lets you export or delete it with /mydata. Each tool has its own privacy policy, and this site has its own.

Which browsers does FanaticalGrabber work in?

Chrome and other Chromium-based browsers, such as Edge and Brave. It installs from a downloaded folder, and the tool's page walks through each step.

Are you part of Fanatical, Steam or Discord?

No. These are independent projects. They work with those services but are not made, approved or supported by them.

Can I buy something here?

Not yet. The shop page is a placeholder while I decide what belongs in it. Nothing on this site takes payments.

How do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Message me on X, or open an issue on the tool's GitHub page: Steam Value Checker or FanaticalGrabber.
